Culture & Notes

Hausa (North)

Hausa cuisine emphasizes grains like millet and rice, savory soups such as Miyan Kuka, grilled meats (Suya), and hearty stews served with "swallows" such as tuwo.

Igbo (East)

Igbo foods feature soups like Ofe Nsala and Oha, fermented ingredients, and dishes like Abacha and Nkwobi — often spicy, smoky, and rich in palm oil flavors.

Yoruba (West)

Yoruba cuisine includes Amala, Ewedu, Gbegiri, and Efo Riro — leafy stews and flavors built on palm oil, peppers, and local vegetables.
